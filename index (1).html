Why claims keep getting denied

The same three reasons show up again and again, and most small practices don't have the staff time to fight every one of them.

01

Eligibility issues

Coverage lapses, plan changes, or mismatched member info that could often have been caught before the claim was even submitted.

02

Missing prior authorization

Auth requirements that shift by payer and plan, easy to miss when you're focused on patient care, not paperwork.

03

Insufficient documentation

Notes that support the treatment clinically but don't spell it out in the specific language a payer is looking for.
